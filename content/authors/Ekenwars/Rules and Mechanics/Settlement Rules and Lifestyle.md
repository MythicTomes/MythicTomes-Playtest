## Settlement Rules and Lifestyle

While staying in a town or settlement, assume room and board is easily found and be paid in tiers. Lifestyles affect character interactions, with higher tiers possibly coming with gameplay rewards.

- Wretched (Free): Live on the streets, making camp with whatever is available. (-1 bonus)
- Poor (1 SP/day): The bare minimum; communal homes and rations for meals. (+0 bonus)
- Modest (1 GP/day): standard private bed and common meals. (+1 bonus)
- Comfortable (2 GP/day): more secure and comfortable accomodation. (+2 bonus)
- Luxurious(5GP/day): premium experience with lavish beds and meals. (+5 bonus)

For animal companions and familiars that require sustenance, the cost is halved.

At the end of a long rest, a creature must make a DC 5 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature gains only a poor rest due to the conditions of their lifestyle. A creature that gains a poor rest recovers half of its expended Hit Dice and regains half of its expended class resources (rounded down) that would normally be restored on a long rest. For example, if a spellcaster would normally regain four 1st-level spell slots, they instead regain two.

A creature gains a bonus to this saving throw based on the lifestyle it maintained during the rest, as shown above.

For each consecutive day a creature maintains a lifestyle of Poor or Wretched, the DC increases by +1. This increase resets when the creature spends at least one day at a higher lifestyle. The maximum DC from this effect is 15.

While camping in the wilderness, creatures are assumed to begin at the Poor lifestyle by default, and the Camp Rest DC for a good rest is 8, excluding weather and environmental conditions. The Camp Rest DC does not increase after multiple days in the wilderness.
