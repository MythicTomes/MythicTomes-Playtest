# Hunting and Foraging Rules

While in the wilderness and without sufficient rations, characters may attempt to hunt or forage for food.

A typical hunt requires at least 4 hours of dedicated activity and cannot be performed alongside other activities such as travel. One creature makes a Wisdom (Survival) check to determine the outcome of the hunt. The DC is 10 + environmental factors, determined by the DM.

On a success, the party gains 1 pound of food, plus 1 additional pound for each point by which the check exceeds the DC. The total amount of food gained cannot exceed the hunter's Wisdom (Survival) modifier (minimum of 1 pound). Each pound of food is sufficient to feed one creature for one Long Rest.

On a failure, no food is gained, and a complication occurs, such as a random encounter or environmental hazard. The DM determines the nature of the complication, either by rolling or choosing from the hunting encounter table.

If a complication interrupts the hunt, the attempt is considered failed. The hunt must be restarted, requiring another 4 hours and a new Survival check.
