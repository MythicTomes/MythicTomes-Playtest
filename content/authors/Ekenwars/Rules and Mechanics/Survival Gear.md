## Survival Gear

Survival gear replaces lifestyle benefits while resting outside a settlement or in the wilderness.

While camping in the wilderness, creatures are assumed to begin at the Poor lifestyle by default, and the Camp Rest DC for a good rest is 8, excluding weather and environmental conditions. The Camp Rest DC does not increase after multiple days in the wilderness.

### General Rules

- Each portion of rations feeds one Small to Medium creature, quadrupled for each size category above Small or Medium.
- Blankets and bedrolls can only be used by one Small or Medium creature.
- Tents can accommodate up to four Small or Medium creatures.
- Any tier of tent counts as covered shelter and cancels weather-imposed disadvantage on Camp Rest saves.
- You may stack lifestyle bonuses from multiple sources (e.g., blanket + bedroll + tent), gaining all applicable bonuses to checks made during the long rest.

### Tiered Equipment

Survival-grade equipment represents standard adventuring gear, as written in the Player's Handbook, and determines the quality of rest in the wild.

#### Survival Grade (PHB Equivalent)

- Basic Rations (5 sp): A creature must consume 2 rations per long rest.
- Blanket (5 sp): You gain +1 to saving throws against weather effects.
- Bedroll (1 gp): You gain +1 to saving throws against weather effects.
- Tent (2 gp): Grants +1 lifestyle bonus. You are mildly protected from environmental exposure, cancel weather-imposed disadvantage on Camp Rest saves while resting inside, and may attempt a Survival check to determine rest quality.

#### Standard Grade

- Standard Rations (1 gp): Grants +1 lifestyle bonus. A creature consumes 1 ration per long rest.
- Standard Blanket (1 gp): Grants +1 lifestyle bonus and +1 to saving throws against extreme cold.
- Standard Bedroll (2 gp): Grants +1 lifestyle bonus. You gain advantage on saving throws against extreme cold.
- Standard Tent (5 gp): Grants +2 lifestyle bonus and advantage on Survival checks made to resist environmental conditions.

#### Premium Grade

- Premium Rations (3 gp): Grants +2 lifestyle bonus. A creature consumes 1 ration per long rest.
- Premium Blanket (1 gp): Grants +2 lifestyle bonus and +2 to saving throws against extreme cold.
- Premium Bedroll (2 gp): Grants +2 lifestyle bonus. You automatically succeed on saving throws against extreme cold.
- Premium Tent (10 gp): Grants +5 lifestyle bonus, advantage on Survival checks, and when making such checks, any roll below 10 is treated as a 10.
