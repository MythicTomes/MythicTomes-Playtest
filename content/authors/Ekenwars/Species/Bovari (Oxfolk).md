## Bovari (Oxfolk)

Bovari are steady, powerful oxfolk whose strength is most evident when a burden must be borne or a path must be broken. Their horns and unstoppable charge make them formidable defenders and laborers alike.

Throughout the Yuanlai Dominion, Bovari are commonly found among fields, farms, and rural settlements, where generations have made use of their exceptional strength and stamina. Many work as farmers, herders, builders, porters, and other laborers whose professions reward endurance and patience.

Others turn that same physical discipline toward more adventurous lives. Bovari monks, guards, wrestlers, caravan protectors, and wandering adventurers are common enough to be familiar sights, often treating strength not merely as a natural gift but as something to be refined through practice and self-control.

Bovari are broad-framed and powerfully built, with thick coats of fur and long hair that they often braid, bind, and decorate with colorful ribbons, beads, cords, and ornaments. Grooming can be a personal expression, a family tradition, or a sign of profession and status.

Their horns are among their most prized features. Many Bovari carve, engrave, paint, or adorn them with decorative patterns and ancestral markings. Over the course of a Bovari's life, these markings may grow into a visual record of important achievements, family ties, beliefs, travels, or personal ideals.

Many Bovari are known for calm, patient temperaments and can appear gentle or unassuming at first meeting. Beneath that composure, however, strong passions are common. Bovari often express themselves openly through performance, competition, feats of strength, craft, or martial discipline, and once roused to action they can be remarkably difficult to stop.

### Bovari Traits

As a Bovari, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Horns.** Your horns are natural melee weapons, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. If you hit with them, you deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier piercing damage, instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
- **Charging Horns.** If you move at least 10 feet straight, you can make an Unarmed Strike with your horns as an Attack Action at the end of that movement. The attack deals an extra 1d6 piercing damage for every 10 feet you moved, up to a maximum of 3d6 extra piercing damage. You can use this feature once on each of your turns.
- **Driving Strike.** When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you can take the Shove action against that creature as part of the attack.
