## Shanyang (Goatfolk)

Shanyang are hardy, sure-footed people known for making homes and roads through places others would consider nearly impassable. Mountain paths, broken ridges, narrow ledges, and steep valleys are familiar terrain to them, and generations of Shanyang have built a reputation for treating dangerous climbs with casual confidence.

Their appearance varies considerably between different families and tribes. Some possess long, sweeping horns that curve dramatically away from the head, while others have shorter, stubbier horns. These differences are often associated with ancestry and regional identity and have become a longstanding source of rivalry, teasing, and occasional scorn between Shanyang communities.

Shanyang are especially famous for scaling impossibly steep mountainsides and navigating difficult terrain with ease. Many carry this confidence into their personalities, developing a dry, mocking sense of humor toward those who struggle to keep pace. Whether the ridicule is playful or entirely sincere often depends on the Shanyang in question.

They commonly travel in close-knit herds and place considerable value on self-reliance, responsibility, and contributing to the group. A Shanyang is often expected to be capable of looking after themselves while still remaining dependable when their community needs them.

Many Shanyang lead physically active lives and pursue martial disciplines as warriors, guards, climbers, or monks. Others channel the same determination toward exploration and spiritual pursuits, seeking out remote temples, dangerous peaks, and difficult journeys as tests of character.

Those with calmer temperaments often become spiritual devotees, guides, teachers, or community leaders. Even among them, however, there is a persistent fascination with difficult goals and seemingly impossible feats, whether that means reaching an untouched summit, mastering a demanding discipline, or leading their people through hardship.

### Shanyang Traits

As a Shanyang, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.
- **Mountainborn.** Your experience navigating alpine heights has given you proficiency in the Athletics and Survival skills.
- **Horns.** Your horns are natural melee weapons, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. If you hit with them, you deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
- **Sure-Footed.** You have advantage on Dexterity saving throws you make to avoid being moved against your will or knocked Prone.
