## Ursaline

Ursalines are powerful, solitary creatures who embody the strength and resilience of the great bears they resemble. They value solitude, self-reliance, and balance, often living in harmony with the wilderness. Calm and composed by nature, Ursalines can become an unstoppable force of fury when provoked, defending themselves and their territory with savage might.

### Ursaline Traits

As an Ursaline, you have these special traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** Your size is Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Age.** Ursalines mature at a similar rate to humans but can live up to 100 years.
- **Alignment.** Ursalines are typically neutral, maintaining a calm and solitary lifestyle. However, when provoked, their wrath is unmatched, and they can act decisively to protect themselves or others.
- **Ursine Nature.** You have proficiency in either Intimidation or Survival (choose when you select this race).
- **Bulk.** Your hit point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 every time you gain a level.
- **Savage Ferocity.** Once per turn, when you make a melee weapon attack or Unarmed Strike, you can add your proficiency bonus as additional damage.
- **Effective Hibernation.** When you expend a Hit Die to regain hit points during a Short Rest, you regain 1 additional hit point for every die expended.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and one language of your choice.
