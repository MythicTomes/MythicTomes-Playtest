## Swinekin (Pigfolk)

Swinekin are broad-built, practical people with a reputation for appetite, resilience, and an uncanny instinct for opportunity. Their traditions often celebrate prosperity in tangible forms: a full table, a well-stocked home, a successful harvest, and the wisdom to recognize value where others might overlook it.

Within the Yuanlai Dominion, Swinekin are commonly associated with Ba Gao, a deity of wealth and prosperity, and many believe this blessing grants them a natural eye for hidden fortune. They are often loud, rowdy, and openly expressive, with communities that place great value on abundance, celebration, and sharing success with others.

### Swinekin Traits

As a Swinekin, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Small or Medium. You choose the size when you select this race.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Bountiful Rest.** When you spend one or more Hit Dice to regain Hit Points at the end of a Short Rest, you regain 1 additional Hit Point for each Hit Die spent. Instead of regaining those Hit Points yourself, you can distribute them among willing creatures within 30 feet of you that have spent at least 1 hour resting. A creature can't regain more Hit Points than its maximum.
- **Subrace.** Choose one of the following subraces.

### Hearthborn

Heartborn Swinekin are smooth-skinned and often associated with food, agriculture, and hospitality. Cuisine holds an important place in many Heartborn communities, where preparing a meal can be an expression of generosity, status, celebration, or affection.

Many Heartborn become merchants, farmers, foragers, cooks, innkeepers, and traders. They are often among the first to organize a feast and just as often among the first to sit down and enjoy it.

- **Versatile Metabolism.** You have proficiency in the Survival skill, and you have advantage on saving throws against poison and disease.
- **Keen Smell.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

### Tuskborn

Tuskborn Swinekin are covered in coarse fur, commonly in shades of brown, grey, or black, and are distinguished by their prominent tusks. They tend to be sturdier and more physically imposing than their Heartborn kin.

Tuskborn have a reputation for strong tempers, fierce pride, and a willingness to meet problems head-on. Many are drawn toward martial professions as warriors, guards, caravan defenders, and soldiers, where their aggression and stubborn endurance can be turned toward protecting their communities.

- **Tusks.** Your tusks are natural melee weapons, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. If you hit with them, you deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier piercing damage, instead of the normal damage and type for an Unarmed Strike.
- **Tusk Charge.** If you move at least 20 feet in a straight line and land an Unarmed Strike using your tusks, you deal an additional 1d8 piercing damage.
