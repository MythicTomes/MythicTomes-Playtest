## Nagarai (Snakefolk)

Nagarai are thoughtful and composed serpentine people associated throughout the Yuanlai Dominion with wisdom, renewal, and quiet ambition. They commonly embody the virtues of patience, careful observation, and an instinct for understanding the world before acting within it. Their measured manner often conceals sharp intelligence, strong convictions, and an ability to endure circumstances that would break others.

Their appearance varies widely between families and regions. Some possess smooth scales in muted greens, browns, greys, or blacks, while others display brighter bands, mottled patterns, or striking markings across the face and body. Their eyes are often keen and unblinking, and many periodically shed portions of their outer scales as they grow. This natural cycle of shedding has made the Nagarai enduring symbols of rebirth, survival, and transformation in Yuanlai culture.

Old traditions claim that the Nagarai were blessed by a great serpentine deity, who granted their ancestors knowledge, cunning, and the wisdom to survive through patience rather than force alone. Because of this association, Nagarai are often regarded as fortunate people, though the same stories have also contributed to a reputation for secrecy and manipulation among those who distrust them.

Nagarai commonly gravitate toward learned and advisory professions. They may be found serving as courtiers, scholars, architects, planners, physicians, administrators, tutors, and political advisers, particularly in communities where foresight and careful judgment are highly valued. Many established households seek Nagarai counsel precisely because of their reputation for patience and long-term thinking.

They also have a strong cultural affinity for herbs, gardens, medicine, and the study of the natural world. Nagarai herbalists and gardeners are respected for their knowledge of useful and dangerous plants alike. This expertise has inevitably given rise to darker associations with toxins and poisoncraft—associations that are not entirely undeserved. Some Nagarai study poisons as medicines, tools of defense, or matters of scholarship, while others have turned that knowledge toward less reputable ends.

Despite the stereotypes surrounding them, Nagarai are no more uniformly secretive or calculating than any other people. What many do share is an appreciation for knowledge, preparation, self-mastery, and the belief that the right action taken at the right moment is often more valuable than strength used too soon.

### Nagarai Traits

As a Nagarai, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 2, and your Intelligence score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Shed.** When you are reduced to half your Hit Point maximum or fewer Hit Points, you can expend one Hit Die and regain Hit Points equal to the number rolled + your Constitution modifier. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
- **Poison Resilience.** You have resistance to poison damage, and you have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
- **Resting Coil.** At the end of a Short or Long Rest, you can gain temporary hit points equal to your Proficiency Bonus + your Constitution modifier. Once you gain temporary hit points with this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
- **Subtle Rebirth.** When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can expend one of your Hit Dice, roll it, and regain Hit Points equal to the number rolled + your Constitution modifier. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
