## Gallian (Horsefolk)

Gallians are humanoids with equine features, long manes, and prominent tails. Their manes are often carefully groomed and decorated with braids, ribbons, beads, cords, or other ornaments. Different families and tribes are known for distinctive textures, lengths, colors, and styles of mane, which can serve as expressions of heritage, fashion, profession, or personal identity.

Within the Yuanlai Dominion, Gallians are widespread and rarely associated with any single region. Their free-spirited nature has made them a common sight along roads, trade routes, rivers, and ports. Tradition holds that they were blessed by a deity of freedom, plains, and winds, and this connection remains an important part of Gallian cultural identity.

Many Gallians are drawn toward occupations that reward speed, stamina, and familiarity with distant places. Couriers, messengers, explorers, caravan workers, sailors, guides, and traveling merchants are common among them.

Gallians have a reputation for being loud, expressive, and boldly competitive. They speak their minds, celebrate openly, and approach contests with considerable enthusiasm. When Gallian communities do settle permanently, they tend to favor broad, open landscapes such as plains, fields, and steppes.

Festivals frequently feature races, endurance contests, athletic competitions, music, and communal celebrations. These events serve as displays of skill and pride, but also as a way of strengthening bonds between families and communities.

### Gallian Traits

As a Gallian, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Dexterity score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet. While you aren't wearing medium or heavy armor, your walking speed increases by 10 feet.
- **Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Hooves.** Your hooves are natural melee weapons, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. If you hit with them, you deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
- **Surefooted Dash.** When you take the Dash action, opportunity attacks made against you have disadvantage, and you ignore difficult terrain until the end of the turn.
- **Fast Pace.** When you travel at a fast pace, you don't have disadvantage on Wisdom checks.

### Gallian Feats

#### Gallian Rider

_Origin feat. Prerequisite: Gallian_

- **Mounting.** A willing Medium or smaller creature within 5 feet of you can mount you by expending movement equal to half its speed. You and the mounted creature occupy the same space.
- **Mounted Limitations.** While a creature is mounted on you, you can take only the Dash, Disengage, or Dodge action. You can take the Attack action only to make Unarmed Strikes.
- **Rider's Weight.** If your rider is Medium or the same size as you, your movement speed is halved.
- **Shared Burden.** When your rider is about to take damage, you can use your Reaction to halve that damage. You take the remaining half of the damage.
