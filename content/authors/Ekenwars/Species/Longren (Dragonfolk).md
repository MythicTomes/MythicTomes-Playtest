## Longren (Dragonfolk)

Longren are an ancient and dignified people said to descend from the heavenly dragons of old. Within the Yuanlai Dominion, they are counted among the recognised Noble Races, carrying with them associations of divine grace, good fortune, authority, and enduring legacy.

Their forms bear the marks of their draconic ancestry. Longren are typically tall and slender, with elongated necks, serpentine features, flowing manes of hair, and scales that can appear in a wide range of vivid colors. Some possess curved fangs, tails, pronounced crests, or other features reminiscent of the divine dragons from which they claim descent.

Longren are long-lived, and many families maintain traditions, offices, estates, or reputations that have endured for centuries. They are often quiet and composed in manner, and tend to become deeply rooted in the communities they inhabit. In many settlements, Longren families are regarded as stabilizing presences whose histories are intertwined with the institutions around them.

Many Longren pursue professions concerned with memory, order, and stewardship. Historians, accountants, magistrates, archivists, administrators, legal scholars, and government officials are common among them, particularly in the older cities of the Dominion. Others withdraw from public life to become monks, wardens, or protectors of remote places, preserving natural sites, ancient traditions, and forgotten histories.

Tradition holds that some Longren bloodlines still maintain unusually strong ties to the heavens, and that deities and heroic spirits look upon them with particular favor. Whether this is divine truth or inherited reputation, most Longren are keenly aware of the weight of ancestry. For many, however, descent alone is not enough. A respected Longren is expected to establish a legacy worthy of remembrance in their own right, rather than relying solely on the accomplishments of those who came before.

### Longren Traits

As a Longren, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Dexterity score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Age.** Though the original dragons are said to be ageless, Longren have been recorded to live for up to 200 years. Many Longren are known to build enduring legacies.
- **Alignment.** Longren tend to respect and embody strong social values, often leaning toward lawful alignments when they settle near civilizations. However, frontier Longren can be as temperamental as nature itself.
- **Divine Luck.** When you fail an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the d20, and you must use the new roll. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
- **Dragonflight.** As a Bonus Action, you manifest the will of your dragon ancestors and borrow their ability for wingless flight and levitation. For 10 minutes, you gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed. Starting at 5th level, you can hover while flying with this trait. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
- **Dragon Lineage.** Choose one of the following lineages.

### Dragon Lineages

#### River

- **Riverborn.** You have a swimming speed equal to twice your walking speed.
- **Amphibious.** You can breathe air and water.

#### Mountain

- **Mountain Sense.** You have Tremorsense with a range of 10 feet. You can also detect the position of precious stones and metals within its range, but you don't detect their nature or quality.
- **Extended Tremorsense.** As a Bonus Action, you can extend the range of your Tremorsense to 60 feet for 10 minutes. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

#### Sky

- **Skyborne Flight.** You can activate your Dragonflight without taking a Bonus Action during your turn. In addition, when you finish a Short Rest, you regain one expended use of Dragonflight.
- **Enduring Flight.** When you use Dragonflight, it lasts for 1 hour.
