## Yanmao (Fowlfolk)

Yanmao are diverse, flightless birdfolk known for their vivid plumage, expressive voices, and strong traditions of performance and ceremony. Their calls can carry song, warning, celebration, rivalry, and emotion, and a skilled voice is often regarded as a source of considerable pride.

Yanmao vary greatly in appearance between families and lineages. Their feathers may be subdued or brilliantly colored, while crests, tail plumes, neck feathers, and other displays differ widely in shape and size. Despite their avian features, Yanmao are flightless and possess no functional wings.

They can be found throughout the Yuanlai Dominion, though many prefer densely populated towns and cities where they can remain close to large communities. Yanmao gatherings are rarely quiet affairs. Conversation, music, calls, and spontaneous displays are common, and public singing or vocal competitions are popular forms of entertainment and prestige.

Their natural grace and expressive nature have given Yanmao a strong presence in theatre, dance, music, ceremonial performance, and martial arts. Many traditions combine deliberate movement with elaborate plumage and carefully controlled vocal calls, turning even disciplined martial practice into a striking public spectacle.

### Yanmao Traits

As a Yanmao, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 2, and your Dexterity score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Birdcall.** You can communicate through a high-frequency birdcall of chirps and whistles unique to you. During a Long Rest, you can teach your birdcall to a number of willing creatures equal to your Proficiency Bonus, provided you share a common language with them. A creature knows your birdcall until you choose to change it at the end of a Long Rest. When you change your birdcall, you can teach the new birdcall during that Long Rest. Creatures that know your birdcall can communicate discreetly with you at a range of 300 feet, provided they can hear the birdcall. To other creatures, the communication sounds like ordinary birds chirping.
- **Sun Sense.** You instinctively know the time of day and when the sun will rise and set.
- **Lineage.** Choose one of the following lineages.

### Dawncaller

Dawncallers carry voices that cut through confusion and command attention at the first sign of danger.

- **Intimidating Call.** You have proficiency in the Intimidation skill.
- **Frightening Squawk.** As an action, you unleash a loud squawk. Choose a number of creatures within 30 feet of you that can hear you. Each target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your Charisma modifier + your Proficiency Bonus) or be Frightened of you until the end of your next turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

### Resplendent

Resplendent Yanmao cultivate elaborate plumage, movement, and presentation to captivate an audience.

- **Performer.** You have proficiency in the Performance skill.
- **Mesmerising Display.** As an action, you make a captivating display. Choose a number of creatures within 30 feet of you that can see you. Each target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your Charisma modifier + your Proficiency Bonus) or be Charmed by you until the end of your next turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

### Fleetfoot

Fleetfoot Yanmao are built for relentless overland travel and bursts of speed across open ground.

- **Athletic Build.** You have proficiency in the Athletics skill.
- **Fleet Runner.** Your walking speed is 35 feet. When you take the Dash action, opportunity attacks made against you have disadvantage until the end of the turn.

### Poised

Poised Yanmao train balance and measured movement until they can hold their ground through sudden force.

- **Graceful Movement.** You have proficiency in the Acrobatics skill.
- **Steady Stance.** You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid being moved against your will or knocked Prone.
