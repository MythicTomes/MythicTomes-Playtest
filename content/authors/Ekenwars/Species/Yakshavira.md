## Yakshavira

Yakshavira are mortals touched by the blessings of the heavenly realms. Such grace may arise from extraordinary virtue, the favor of a deity or heroic spirit, or an inheritance passed through a bloodline that has carried divine influence for generations. Whatever its origin, a Yakshavira bears a spark of something greater than themselves.

Their heavenly nature often manifests as an unmistakable radiance. Some possess luminous eyes, sacred markings, faint halos, unusually vivid features, or voices and movements accompanied by subtle supernatural phenomena. Others appear almost entirely mortal until their divine power is called forth.

Long hair is especially significant among many Yakshavira. It is traditionally associated with long life, enduring wisdom, and the continuation of heavenly blessings. Many grow their hair throughout their lives and develop particular ways of tying, braiding, knotting, or ornamenting it. These styles may reflect family traditions, spiritual vows, personal achievements, or the nature of the blessing they carry.

Yakshavira rarely pass entirely unnoticed within the Yuanlai Dominion. Their presence is commonly regarded as a favorable sign from the heavens, and communities may treat them with admiration, curiosity, or reverence. Some readily embrace this attention, becoming celebrated warriors, performers, local heroes, or public figures whose deeds reinforce their heavenly reputation.

Others find such expectations burdensome and choose quieter lives as wanderers, pilgrims, explorers, or guardians far from settled communities. A heavenly blessing does not necessarily bring certainty of purpose, and some Yakshavira spend much of their lives determining what, if anything, the heavens expect of them.

Many Yakshavira possess an instinctive sensitivity toward spirits and the natural energies of the world. This affinity draws some toward monasteries, temples, secluded shrines, or traditions of mysticism and spiritual cultivation. Yet divine ancestry does not make a Yakshavira inherently virtuous, pious, or gentle. Some reject organized faith entirely, while others wield their inherited radiance according to ambitions wholly their own.

Ultimately, the Yakshavira are defined not simply by heavenly favor, but by what they choose to make of it.

### Yakshavira Traits

As a Yakshavira, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Small or Medium. You choose the size when you select this species.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Darkvision.** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
- **Yaksha Lineage.** Choose one of the lineages below. You gain the features of that lineage and the benefit of your _Yaksha Manifestation_.
- **Yaksha Manifestation.** As a Bonus Action, you manifest your lineage for 1 minute. The manifestation's benefit is determined by your Yaksha Lineage. You can end the manifestation early during your turns (no action required), or when you are Incapacitated. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Yaksha Lineages

#### Ascended

Your ancestor was a saint, heroic spirit, ascended god, or other being of luminous divinity.

- **Radiant Soul.** You have Resistance to Radiant damage.
- **Ascended Manifestation.** When you activate your Yaksha Manifestation, you emit Bright Light in a 20-foot radius centered on yourself. At the start of each of your turns, one creature of your choice in the light gains temporary hit points equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

#### Natural Spirit

Your ancestor was an elemental, guardian spirit, or transcendent being of the living world.

- **Spirit Aspect.** Choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder. You have Resistance to the chosen damage type, and you know the _Elementalism_ cantrip. You may use Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as its spellcasting ability (choose when you select this lineage).
- **Natural Manifestation.** For the duration of your Yaksha Manifestation, when a creature ends its turn within 5 feet of you, it takes damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. The damage type is the same as your Spirit Aspect.

#### Demon

Your ancestor was a wrathful divine being whose fearsome form was a force of nature.

- **Wrathful Form.** Choose claws, horns, or fangs. These traits manifest in your physical form. Your chosen form is a natural weapon that you can use to make Unarmed Strikes, dealing 1d8 + your Strength modifier slashing, bludgeoning, or piercing damage respectively, instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
- **Demonic Manifestation.** For the duration of your Yaksha Manifestation, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC, and you gain temporary hit points equal to your Proficiency Bonus at the start of each of your turns.
