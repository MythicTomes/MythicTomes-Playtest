## Gouren (Houndfolk)

Gouren are perceptive, sociable houndfolk known for their alertness, energy, and strong sense of companionship. Their communities often value mutual aid, quick coordination, and being present when others need them.

Gouren are highly varied in appearance. They come in many statures, builds, coat lengths, colors, ear shapes, and facial features, with different families and communities displaying markedly different traits. Their temperaments are equally diverse, though many Gouren are known for being expressive, loyal, and quick to form strong social bonds.

They are commonly drawn to busy settlements and large communities, where their active and social nature can thrive. Dense cities, markets, ports, and trade centers often have sizeable Gouren populations, and many become deeply involved in neighborhood life.

Gouren frequently find work as watchkeepers, guards, guides, messengers, couriers, attendants, and other professions that benefit from vigilance, reliability, and familiarity with people. Many develop broad networks of friends, coworkers, and acquaintances, and are often valued for their ability to notice trouble early and rally others quickly.

Though Gouren are sometimes stereotyped as excitable or overly eager, these traits often reflect a culture that places great importance on enthusiasm, participation, and loyalty. For many Gouren, belonging to a community means taking an active role in protecting, supporting, and celebrating it.

### Gouren Traits

As a Gouren, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Small or Medium. You choose the size when you select this race.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Expedient Aid.** You can take the Search or Help action as a Bonus Action.
- **Quick Retrieval.** On each of your turns, you can interact with up to two objects for free, but only to retrieve or drop an object.
- **Allied Focus.** When you make an attack roll or ability check while an ally is within 5 feet of you, you can choose to have advantage on the roll. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
