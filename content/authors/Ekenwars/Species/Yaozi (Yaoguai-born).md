## Yaozi (Yaoguai-born)

Yaozi are people marked by curses, profane influence, or inherited spiritual corruption. Some were transformed by their own dealings with dangerous spirits or malevolent deities, others were cursed through no choice of their own, and some are born into bloodlines already touched by such forces.

Their appearance often makes this affliction difficult to hide. Many Yaozi possess unnaturally dark, slick hair, luminous or strangely colored eyes, scarred or discolored skin, and markings that resemble burns, brands, veins, or ritual scars. Their complexions can take on striking shades of blue, purple, green, or other unnatural hues, while fangs, nails, horns, or other features may grow in distorted or exaggerated forms.

Because these traits are widely associated with curses and dangerous entities, Yaozi are often treated with suspicion throughout the Yuanlai Dominion. Many communities regard them as unstable, unlucky, or spiritually dangerous, regardless of the circumstances that caused their condition. This prejudice can make even ordinary social life difficult, and many Yaozi grow accustomed to scrutiny, avoidance, or outright hostility.

As a result, some live solitary lives on the margins of larger communities, while others form small enclaves with people who share similar experiences. A Yaozi may spend years attempting to understand, suppress, cleanse, or simply coexist with the curse that shaped them.

Not every Yaozi views their condition as something to be cured. Some embrace the power or identity it has given them, while others reject the entities, ancestors, or circumstances responsible for it entirely. For many, life is defined less by the curse itself than by the effort required to build a place in a society that often judges them before knowing who they are.

### Yaozi Traits

As a Yaozi, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Alignment.** Yaozi tend toward chaotic alignments, whether through the nature of their curse or the circumstances of their birth.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Wrathful Presence.** As an action, you can unleash a monstrous visage. Choose a number of creatures within 30 feet of you that can see or hear you. Each target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Charisma modifier) or have the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
- **Yaoguai Resilience.** You have advantage on saving throws to detect and resist curses.
- **Fearless.** You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
- **Body Morph.** As an action, you can contort your body in numerous ways. You can change your skin color or hair length, enlarge or remove your teeth, change your eye color, or manifest spectral markings on your skin. You can't grow additional appendages, and your form retains its shape and number of limbs. The alteration lasts for 1 minute, or until you become Incapacitated.
