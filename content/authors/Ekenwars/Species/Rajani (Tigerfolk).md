## Rajani (Tigerfolk)

Rajani are regal and formidable tigerfolk, known for their commanding presence, fierce passions, and strong sense of personal dignity. Rajani tend to carry themselves with confidence and purpose, whether as respected heirs, wandering adventurers, devoted protectors, ambitious merchants, or individuals seeking to define their own legacy

Rajani vary considerably in appearance, often according to family, regional, or tribal heritage. Their coats may range from deep orange and tawny gold to pale cream or white, but most bear distinctive dark stripes across the body, face, and limbs. These markings often soften with age, becoming lighter or less defined over time. Some elderly Rajani eventually develop coats that appear almost entirely uniform in color, with only faint traces of their former stripes remaining.

They are powerfully built, with broad shoulders, strong limbs, and prominent claws, yet their size can be deceptive. Rajani move with remarkable balance and control, capable of remaining quiet even across difficult terrain. This combination of strength and stealth has made them well suited to professions involving security, defense, scouting, hunting, and protection.

Within the Yuanlai Dominion, Rajani are considered one of the Noble Tribes, a reputation born from their imposing presence, martial traditions, and association with discipline and authority. Rajani communities are often tightly knit, and loyalty to family, companions, and trusted communities is taken seriously. Outsiders may find them reserved at first, but those who earn their trust are often welcomed with equal intensity.

Many established Rajani families hold respected positions in the Dominion's larger cities, serving as lawkeepers, merchant houses, military officers, and spiritual advisers. Others remain closer to the frontier, where organized family companies work as mercenaries, explorers, caravan guards, hunters, and scouts.

Across these roles, Rajani traditions tend to prize strength, courage, patience, and discipline. Whether guarding a city gate, stalking prey through the wilderness, or negotiating on behalf of a powerful household, a Rajani is expected to act with purpose and composure.

### Rajani Traits

As a Rajani, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 35 feet, and you have a climbing speed of 35 feet.
- **Claws.** Your claws are natural melee weapons, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. If you hit with them, you deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage, instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
- **Stalker.** You have proficiency in the Stealth skill.
- **Mark Prey.** As a Bonus Action, you can mark one creature you can see within 60 feet of you as your prey for 10 minutes. You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks to track your prey. Once per turn, when you deal damage to your prey with a weapon attack, you deal additional damage equal to your Strength modifier. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Short or Long Rest.
