## Ratfolk

Once fabled to have won the great Zodiac Race, Ratfolk are said to have been blessed with ingenuity, adaptability, and uncommon fortune. Whether or not the old stories are true, Ratfolk have earned a reputation throughout the Yuanlai Dominion as resourceful people capable of making a life almost anywhere.

Ratfolk can be found in nearly every earthly profession. They work as traders, scavengers, excavators, miners, explorers, merchants, laborers, and craftspeople, often gravitating toward work that rewards persistence, curiosity, and quick thinking. Wherever there is opportunity to be found—or work that others have overlooked—a Ratfolk is rarely far away.

Ratfolk communities traditionally place great value on cooperation, shared responsibility, and communal identity. Families and neighbors commonly pool resources and support one another through difficult times. Even so, Ratfolk are far from uniform. Many leave their communities to pursue individual ambitions, travelling great distances in search of work, fortune, knowledge, or a purpose entirely their own.

### Ratfolk Traits

As a Ratfolk, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Darkvision.** You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
- **Keen Senses.** You have proficiency in the Perception and Stealth skills.
- **Keen Smell.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
- **Lineage.** Choose one of the following lineages.

### Skitterling

Skitterlings are small and lightly built, with soft coats that commonly range through pale browns, creams, greys, and whites. Their compact frames and quick movements make them naturally suited to crowded streets, workshops, kitchens, stages, and other places where agility is valued.

Hardworking and sociable, Skitterlings are especially common within the Yuanlai Dominion's theatres, inns, restaurants, markets, and hospitality trades. Their communities often prize attentiveness and cooperation, though many Skitterlings are equally comfortable relying on their own quick wits when travelling alone.

- **Size.** You are Small.
- **Small Frame.** You can occupy the space of a creature at least one size larger than you. While you occupy that space, you have disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws, and other creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against you.
- **Skitter Away.** When a creature ends its turn within 5 feet of you, you can use your Reaction to move up to half your movement speed. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

### Warrenward

Warrenwards are larger and more heavily built than their Skitterling kin. Their coats tend toward darker shades of brown, grey, and black, and their sturdy bodies are known for remarkable resilience.

Many Warrenwards are drawn toward professions carried out away from public attention: mining, excavation, warehousing, night work, underground construction, and other trades where patience and comfort in darkness are valuable. Some prefer quiet homes and workplaces removed from the bustle of larger settlements.

Old stereotypes sometimes portray Warrenwards as secretive, calculating, or distrustful. In practice, their caution and eye for opportunity have helped many become respected merchants, investors, and business owners. Successful Warrenwards are also known for reinvesting their fortunes into the families, neighborhoods, and communities that supported them.

- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Moving Reflexes.** If you have moved during your turn, you have advantage on Dexterity saving throws until the start of your next turn.
- **Hardy Constitution.** You have advantage on saving throws against poison and disease. Once per Long Rest, when you fail such a saving throw, you can choose to succeed instead.
