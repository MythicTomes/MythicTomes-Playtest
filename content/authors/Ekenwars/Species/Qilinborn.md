## Qilinborn

Qilinborn are graceful, horned people whose ancestry is traced to benevolent nature spirits and the auspicious qilin of old tales. Their presence is often associated with peace, good fortune, and harmony. Within the Yuanlai Dominion, they are counted among the recognised Noble Races, and many traditions regard the arrival of a Qilinborn family as a favorable omen.

Their appearance is delicate and striking. Qilinborn possess pale coats that often appear in soft shades of cream, light blue, muted gold, or other washed pastel hues. Their long manes can appear almost translucent in certain light, and many also possess tails, cloven hooves, and fine whiskers.

Male Qilinborn are often larger and more powerfully built, with leonine features, long leathery whiskers, and a pair of branching antlers. Female Qilinborn tend to be more slender in frame, with features often compared to deer or horses, and possess a single straight horn rising from the forehead. These traits vary in size and shape between families, but remain some of the most recognizable distinctions among Qilinborn.

It is commonly said that peaceful times follow wherever Qilinborn settle. Whether this is the lingering influence of their spiritual ancestry or simply a reflection of their way of life, many Qilinborn communities are found in quiet villages and remote settlements surrounded by forests, rivers, and open countryside.

Those who live in larger towns and cities often favor domestic, civic, or spiritual roles. Qilinborn are frequently found serving as mediators, diplomats, healers, teachers, temple attendants, and community leaders, where their patient manner and preference for peaceful resolution are highly valued.

Their reputation for gentleness can sometimes be mistaken for weakness or timidity. In practice, many Qilinborn simply prefer to resolve conflict through restraint, patience, and careful judgment before resorting to force.

### Qilinborn Traits

As a Qilinborn, you have the following traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Charisma score increases by 1.
- **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
- **Size.** You are Medium.
- **Speed.** Your walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Horn.** Your horn is a natural melee weapon, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. If you hit with it, you deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier piercing or radiant damage (your choice when you make the attack), instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
- **Gentle Spirit.** You have advantage on saving throws against being Charmed.
- **Qilin Magic.** You know the _Guidance_ cantrip. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast _Healing Word_ with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can also cast _Calm Emotions_ with this trait. Once you cast either spell with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it again until you finish a Long Rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
