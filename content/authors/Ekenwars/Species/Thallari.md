## Thallari

Thallari are enigmatic beings formed from pure elemental energy rather than born in the conventional sense. They emerge fully formed, each with an individual consciousness and will, though the exact process by which they come into existence remains poorly understood.

Many Thallari are naturally amorphous, but most adopt slender humanoid forms when interacting with mortals. Even then, their bodies rarely appear entirely solid. Their shapes can seem to flicker, ripple, or shift according to mood, with limbs, hair, and other features blending into one another as though sculpted from moving flame, water, mist, stone, lightning, or other elemental matter. Artists often struggle to depict them clearly, as their forms create an impression of constant motion even while standing still.

A faint radiance surrounds many Thallari, and their bodies are often lightly bioluminescent. This glow may brighten, dim, or change with emotion, and can make their presence unmistakable in darkness.

Despite their unusual origins, Thallari possess a profound connection to the living world. They are resilient, adaptable, and deeply attuned to natural cycles, often forming bonds with plants, animals, spirits, and landscapes. In this way, they frequently serve as living bridges between the elemental and material realms.

> [!info] Setting note: Hongshan
> In Hongshan, Thallari are commonly understood as sentient forces of will birthed by the Heavenly Realms or by the deities that dwell within them. Some appear to serve as divine observers, guardians, or sentries, carrying out purposes known only to themselves and the powers that created them. Others seem less directed, wandering the mortal world as independent manifestations of elemental or natural forces.
>
> Residents of the Yuanlai Dominion strongly revere the Thallari as an uncommon and divine occurence, and their presence is mostly met with fear or awe, particularly when the nature of their purpose is unclear.
>
> Most Thallari are encountered far from dense settlements, drawn instead toward forests, mountains, rivers, ruins, and other places where the natural and spiritual worlds overlap. There they may commune with local spirits, calm violent supernatural disturbances, protect isolated places, or simply guide travelers who have strayed into dangerous lands.

As a Thallari, you have these special traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 1, and another ability score of your choice (Wisdom, Intelligence, or Charisma) increases by 2.
- **Creature Type.** You are an Elemental.
- **Size.** Thallari are comparable in size to humans, ranging between 5 and 7 feet tall. Your size is Medium.
- **Speed.** 30 feet
- **Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can’t discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Elemental Physiology.** Upon selecting this race, choose one elemental type: fire, cold, acid, lightning, or poison. You gain the following benefits:
  - **Elemental Resistance.** You gain resistance to damage of the chosen type.
  - **Elemental Magic.** You know the Elementalism cantrip. In addition, you know one cantrip based on the element you chose for your Elemental Resistance:
    - **Acid.** Acid Splash
    - **Cold.** Ray of Frost
    - **Fire.** Fire Bolt
    - **Lightning.** Shocking Grasp
    - **Poison.** Poison Spray
      Your spellcasting ability for these cantrips is Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (choose when you select this race).
  - **Elemental Diet.** You do not need to eat or drink. Instead, you sustain yourself by absorbing a substantial source of your Elemental Physiology type, as determined by the DM. You must do so at least once each week or gain 1 level of exhaustion. For example, a Fire Thallari can absorb a burning campfire, while a Cold Thallari can consume a pound of ice.
- **Armor of the Elements.** Your unique elemental physiology prevents you from wearing conventional armor. While unarmored, your AC equals 13 + your proficiency bonus. In addition, when you are about to take damage, you can use your reaction to gain temporary hit points equal to twice your proficiency bonus. You can use this reaction a number of times equal to the ability modifier as your Elemental Magic trait, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Primordial.
