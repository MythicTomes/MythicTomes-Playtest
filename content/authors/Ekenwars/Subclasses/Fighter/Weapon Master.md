## Weapon Master (Fighter Subclass)

A weapon master adapts fluidly in combat, switching weapons and techniques to exploit openings and maintain relentless pressure.

### Level 3: Compact Arsenal

You possess a specially designed weapon roll that allows you to store and access multiple weapons with ease. The weapon roll can store up to six weapons that lack the heavy, two-handed, or reach properties. While stored in the roll, these weapons appear concealed and indistinguishable from a normal traveling bundle.

As an object interaction, you can draw or stow up to two weapons from the roll.

Additionally, you can draw or stow one weapon as part of each attack you make during the Attack action.

### Level 3: Flow of Arms

You use your improved weapon techniques to switch weapons to confuse the enemy and gain an advanatage to pierce through their defenses.

Once per turn, when you hit with a weapon attack using a different weapon, you gain one of the following benefits:

- The attack deals extra damage equal to your proficiency bonus.
- You gain advantage on the attack roll.

### Level 7: Adaptive Fighter

You gain an additional fighting style. It must be one of the following: Archery, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Interception, Thrown Weapon Fighting, or Two-Weapon Fighting.

Additionally, when you make a weapon attack, you can change its damage type to bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing, as you use various parts of the weapon for the strike.

### Level 10: Relentless Counter

When a creature within your reach hits you with a weapon attack, you can use your reaction to make one weapon attack against that creature.

Additionally, when you use your Tactical Master feature, you can choose from the following masteries as well: Topple, Vex, and Graze.

### Level 15: Flowing Assault

When you take the Attack action, you can trigger Flow of Arms twice on your turn, instead of once.

Additionally, when you hit a creature with an attack empowered by Flow of Arms, you can move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Level 18: Perfect Flow

You gain both benefits of Flow of Arms whenever it triggers.

When you hit with an attack empowered by Flow of Arms, you gain a +2 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.

Additionally, when you use Relentless Counter, you can make two weapon attacks instead of one.
