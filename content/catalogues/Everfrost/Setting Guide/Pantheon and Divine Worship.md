## Pantheon and Divine Worship

- Oath of blaze patrons. Deity/faith of flame and hope. 2 sects; both may or may not be the same deity with focused aspects :
  - Svarog - God of Fire and the Forge: pyromaniac fanatics, and defenders of life. Followers are Firekeepers: Fire is power and the winter is evil and we must abolish all those who corrupt life's purpose.
  - Zorya: Goddess of comfort of life. Dawnkeeper. Followers are Lanterns: Fire is warming and comforting and gathers people together and it's a symbol of hope and life and it must be protected
- Vels - God of Beasts and Boundaries. Animals in the wild survive for good reason; perhaps a deity blessed them to be this resilient. Worshiped by shamans and druids. Druidic portals blurring the boundary of man, beast, wild, and fey. God of transitional change (weather, life and death).
- ice fey/demons. Storm came from somewhere. People believe Must be divine punishment or great plan. Regardless, pray for the winds to be less harsh while travelling, and punish our enemies
- Marzanna - Goddess of winter, death, and dreams. Former keeper of cyclic seasons; abrupted because now it's winter forever. Gaining prevelence as the deity of Eternity and Sorrow due to the Everfrost Blizzard.
- Morovik - God of Pestilence and Hunger. Associated with undead, ghouls, vampires, draugr, plague.
- Domovoi - Patron of the Hearth: domestic blessing, protection, food.
