Campaign prompt and knowledge:

- The central government is controlled by the tsar. Government working on large project and diverting massive resources to the capital.
- Blizzard has lasted at least 300 years. Prior to this, the country (pending name) was an industrial nation (soviet era industrialism). They have magitech and locomotives.
- Several dozen settlements remain; villages and Hamlets in the outskirts of the country. Less than 10 major cities remain, excluding the central capital. The closer you are the the capital, the stronger their hold over resources are. This is a double edged sword; you gain more resources but your autonomy is owned by the central government and are subject to continuous monitoring. Edge villages are cared for the least and most have to fend for themselves but are still expected to repair and maintain supply lines for government support (many depend on energy and good from central sources)
- Magic is relatively rare. It is partly why the original government invested heavily on magitech equipment and alternative sources of energy. Magic practitioners are recruited and trained and well respected in their communities, but also feared
